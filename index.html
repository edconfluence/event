About the Summit

Where education leaders set down the day-to-day and talk about what's next

EDCONFLUENCE brings founders, principals, college and university leaders, and academic directors from across India's education ecosystem into one room for a focused day — covering the full journey of learners aged 12 to 28, from school through higher education.

No trade-show floor. No open registration. Every session is built around one question: what does an institution need to do differently, starting Monday morning? The conclave closes with the EDCONFLUENCE Awards, recognising the institutions and individuals raising the bar for young learners.

Agenda & Keynotes

Five conversations that matter

Built around real time with educators — not a lecture schedule. Every session moves toward one thing: what a leader can act on when they get back to campus.

SESSION 01
Day 1 · Morning
Opening Keynote — The Next Decade of Indian Education
A framing address on where education for ages 12 to 28 is headed in India — enrolment shifts, policy change, and what "future-ready" actually means for today's learners.
SESSION 02
Day 1 · Midday
The Educator's Roundtable — What's Actually Hard Right Now
An open, closed-door conversation with school principals, college deans and academic directors about the pressures they carry: staffing, budgets, institutional expectations, and parents and students who want everything, immediately.
SESSION 03
Day 1 · Afternoon
Technology in Learning — Where It Actually Helps
A practical look at AI-assisted teaching, adaptive learning platforms, and institutional ERPs — and an honest discussion of where technology has overpromised, across schools and campuses alike.
SESSION 04
Day 1 · Afternoon
Fireside Chat — Leading Change Without Losing the Mission
Founders and academic leaders on scaling an institution, managing growth, and holding onto why it was started in the first place.
SESSION 05
Day 1 · Evening
EDCONFLUENCE Awards Ceremony
An evening recognising the institutions, educators and young changemakers driving genuine change — closing the conclave on a note of celebration.

Challenges Facing Schools Today

  • Attracting and retaining good teachers and faculty
  • Rising infrastructure and compliance costs
  • Mental health and wellbeing from adolescence to early adulthood
  • Bridging the digital divide across institutions
  • Meeting institutional, parent and regulatory expectations at once

Where Technology Can Help

  • AI-assisted lesson planning and grading
  • Adaptive, personalised learning paths for every age group
  • Institutional ERP for admissions, fees and attendance
  • Data dashboards for academic decision-making
  • Hybrid classrooms for smaller or remote campuses
